# EVSELECT Brand & Design System Guidelines

## Brand Overview
- **Brand Name**: EVSELECT™
- **Tagline**: "DRIVE BETTER. SELECT SMARTER."
- **Target Market**: Thailand EV Owners (Right-Hand Drive / สเปกไทย RHD)
- **Primary Value Proposition**: 100% Guaranteed Fitment QC testing on Thai-spec EV models (BYD, Tesla, Zeekr, Deepal, Geely, MG).

## UI/UX & Color System (Modern White Theme)
1. **Background**: Pure White (`#ffffff`) for root canvas and primary cards; `bg-slate-50` for alternating section backgrounds and subtle containers.
2. **Typography & Text Color**:
   - Primary Headings & High Contrast Text: Slate 900 (`#0f172a` / `#020617`).
   - Secondary / Subtitles: Slate 600 (`#475569`) or Slate 500 (`#64748b`).
   - Font: Prompt (Thai/Latin sans) with clean weights (400, 500, 600, 700, 800).
3. **Accent Colors**:
   - Lime Green (`#84cc16` / `#65a30d`): Used for QC Fitment badges, review rating scorecard highlights, and primary CTA buttons (`bg-lime-500 hover:bg-lime-400 text-black font-bold`).
   - Shopee Orange (`bg-orange-50 text-orange-600 border border-orange-200` or `bg-orange-500` CTA).
   - Social / LINE / Facebook Blue (`bg-blue-50 text-blue-600 border border-blue-200` or `bg-blue-600` CTA).
4. **Cards & Containers**:
   - Border: Subtle light slate (`border-slate-200` or `border-slate-200/90`).
   - Shadows: Soft, modern shadows (`shadow-xs` / `shadow-sm` up to `shadow-md` on hover).
   - Header: Translucent glassmorphism (`bg-white/90 backdrop-blur-md border-b border-slate-200/90`).

## Editorial Review Standards (EVSELECT Standards)
1. **12-Dimension Evaluation**: Performance (0-100 km/h), Range & Real Consumption, Thai Hot Climate (40°C) AC Performance, FSD/Suspension on Thai roads, Interior build, Charging speed, Fitment accessories, Verdict Score.
2. **Image Requirements**: Always use clean, authentic exterior, cockpit, and details photography optimized with Next.js `<Image>`.
