## Reader problem and corrections

The old rendered article promised a perfect Thai-road setup, assigned unsupported click counts and spring rates to five EV models, and presented an invented three-case travel diagram as engineering evidence. It also claimed a 60% ride improvement, universal minimum travel/clearance/ADAS thresholds, zero cavitation, and a lowering amount that would preserve safe flood driving. The actual old page was opened and its full accessibility content inspected before replacement; no old-version reader approval is claimed.

The revised opening describes a passenger feeling a bridge-joint impact followed by body oscillation on a wavy road. It tells a first-time reader what they will gain: distinguish the symptom, collect useful information, and discuss necessary work before paying for an entire suspension set. It makes no promise that one damper architecture or universal setting cures every EV.

Removed the unsupported numeric recipes, fabricated travel diagram, test/score claims, aggressive-driving table and flooded-road assurance. Replaced them with three initial questions, separate symptom descriptions, manufacturer tyre/load guidance, an explicitly scoped Model 3 Performance factory-mode example, installer questions, and a recorded one-variable-at-a-time comparison process. The body points to alignment, tyres/coilovers, shock construction and damper adjustment at the relevant sentences. There is no duplicated damper graph. Brand text in the article, card and shared layout links home; metadata and alt text remain plain text.

The old catalogue Lamborghini image and technical/rating claims were replaced with the same identified Model 3 Performance photograph, appropriate source/license credit, six-minute reading estimate, concise description and fitment questions. Title, description, dates and cover now come from shared article data, including sitemap lastModified.
