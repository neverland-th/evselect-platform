## Requested outcome

- Explain rotor diameter, thickness and working radius; distinguish brake torque, one-stop grip limits and repeated heat load.
- Compare plain/slotted/drilled faces separately from internal ventilation and material.
- Explain why horsepower is not a universal brake fitment or safety rating; provide conditional usage-oriented power bands.
- Give value-oriented maintenance/pad/iron-disc/BBK alternatives alongside a fair case for premium CCB.
- Examine Thai downhill context and the 1,000°C claim without inventing measured temperatures.
- Intersperse actual manufacturer product imagery and a correctly credited Thai-context photo.
- Add Japanese ENDLESS options and source-verified photographs (user steering).
- Add clickable circular comparisons for performance, price and dust by product/brand (user steering).
- Validate and deploy the finished frontend change under standing approval.

## Inaccurate or unsupported original statements removed

- EVs above an arbitrary weight necessarily need CCB.
- CCB guarantees zero fade, never distorts, or is the only suitable EV track option.
- All road CCB must be deliberately warmed like carbon–carbon racing brakes.
- One kilogram of unsprung weight universally equals several kilograms of sprung mass.
- Universal long CCB lifetime, unverified Thai price bands, and unqualified DOT 5.1 upgrade advice.
- Empty storytelling widget; misleading old listing highlights and metadata.

## Interactive content contract

Energy lab: translational kinetic energy and gravitational potential energy only. No brake-temperature or stopping-distance prediction. Regeneration percentage, mass and descent are hypothetical; drag, rolling losses, wheel rotation, brake distribution, thermal partition and cooling are explicitly omitted. A 2,200 kg / 100 km/h reference is 0.848765 MJ, 120 km/h is 1.222222 MJ (+44%); 1,000 m descent is 21.582 MJ. Heat-capacity example inputs are illustrative and never extrapolated to a number of corners to reach 1,000°C.

Circular comparison: ENDLESS SSM PLUS / MX72 / MX72 PLUS, Project μ TYPE PS and Brembo EV Kit. The thermal ring shows maker-declared pad working disc-temperature ceiling on an explicitly labelled 0–800°C axis; it is NOT a brake-performance score. Dust has qualitative statements or missing data and no fabricated numeric arc. Price starts empty, uses user-entered quotes and an explicitly hypothetical adjustable budget. Different quote scopes are gated; price state is keyed by product and scope. Missing evidence is represented by dashes, never zero ratings. No combined score or winning-brand claim.

All content and default reference outputs render without JavaScript; calculation/select controls are hidden with an explanatory no-script message. FAQ uses native details. No new chart package or third-party widget is required.
